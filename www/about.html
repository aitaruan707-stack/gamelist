About Tapzens

Tapzens is a free online games portal where you can instantly play curated puzzle, action and arcade games right in your browser — no downloads, no sign-ups, no friction.

Our Mission

We believe great casual games should be one tap away. Our mission is to build the fastest, friendliest place to play HTML5 games on any device — phones, tablets, or desktops — without installs, pop-ups, or paywalls.

What We Do

  • Hand-pick and curate high-quality casual games across puzzle, sorting, match-3, action and arcade.
  • Optimize every page for instant load and smooth mobile performance.
  • Keep the experience free, lightweight, and respectful of your time and data.
