Where to start

If you play the sorting games — water glasses, screws, shelves and stacks — the genre guide is the one that pays off fastest, because all four run on the same constraint. If you keep losing match-3 levels with two pieces left, the move-budget article is about how the stages are priced rather than how sharp your eye is.

Parents and anyone cautious about free game sites should read the safety and family guides first: they describe the advertising, the consent prompt and the in-game shop buttons exactly as they are.
