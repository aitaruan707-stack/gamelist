About Arrow Maze Solve

Place and rotate arrows to guide a rolling ball through tricky mazes to the goal. Solve clever routing puzzles across hand-crafted levels.

If you enjoy puzzle games, you're in the right place — Arrow Maze Solve is optimized for touch and keyboard alike, loads in seconds and stays free to play forever.

Screenshot

How to Play

  1. Tap arrows to rotate them.
  2. Press Play to launch the ball through your route.
  3. Guide the ball to the goal without falling off.

Features

  • Instant play in your browser — no download or install.
  • Easy to learn, hard to master gameplay.
  • Relaxing visuals and deeply satisfying feedback.
  • Optimized for phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Free forever — no paywalls, no forced sign-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Arrow Maze Solve free?

Yes, completely free to play in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it is optimized for touch and landscape screens.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required — just press Play and it loads instantly.

Progress is saved in your browser only, so clearing browser data may reset it.
