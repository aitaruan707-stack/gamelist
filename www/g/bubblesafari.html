About Bubble Safari

Aim, match, and pop colorful bubbles on a jungle adventure. Clear the board with satisfying chain reactions and climb through hundreds of levels.

Bubble Safari is optimized for touch and keyboard alike, loads in seconds and stays free to play forever — no download and no sign-up. If you enjoy bubble, match-3, casual games, you can play Bubble Safari online right now in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop.

How to Play

  1. Drag to aim, release to shoot.
  2. Match 3 or more of the same color to pop.
  3. Drop hanging bubbles for bonus points.
