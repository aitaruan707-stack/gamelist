WELCOME TO YOUR HAPPY PLACE

Free online games.
Room for every kind of player.

Zupilo is a collection of 20 free browser games for life’s little breaks. Match tiles, sort colors, solve puzzles or jump into arcade action. Every game plays on mobile and desktop, with no downloads or account required.

Start with puzzle games for a relaxed challenge, try action games for a change of pace, or go for one more round in our arcade. Each game has its own controls, screenshots and playing guide.

BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

A few things to know

Are the games on Zupilo really free?

Yes — every game is free to play in your browser with no downloads, no sign-ups and no paywalls. Zupilo is supported without ever charging players.

Do I need to install anything to play?

No. All games run instantly in your browser on phones, tablets and desktops. If it loads, it plays.

Can I play Zupilo games on my phone?

Absolutely. Every game is touch-optimized and mobile-friendly, from portrait puzzle games to landscape arcade action.

How often are new games added?

We add new curated games regularly — check the news page or come back often for the latest releases.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No accounts, ever. Favorites and recently played games are saved privately in your own browser.

Who are Zupilo games designed for?

Zupilo is a general-audience games site intended for players aged 13 and over, and it is not directed at children under 13. There is no chat, no payment prompts and no external downloads, and ad personalization can be switched off at any time from Manage consent.

How do my favorites and progress work?

They live only in your browser's local storage — private to your device, and removable anytime by clearing site data.

Can I play offline?

Zupilo lives in your browser, so you'll need a connection — but every game loads in seconds and never needs an install.
