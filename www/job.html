Careers at Tapzens

We're a small, remote-first team on a mission to make casual games effortless to play. Join us and help millions of players tap into fun — everywhere, on any device.

How We Work

  • Remote-first and async-friendly — we measure outcomes, not hours.
  • A small senior team where your work ships to real players fast.
  • Owner mindset: you take features and products from idea to launch.
  • Players first: every decision starts from what makes the game and portal better.
  • Keep it light: our pages, our games and our processes stay fast and simple.

Open Roles

HTML5 Game Developer (Cocos / Phaser)

Remote · Full-time

Build lightweight, performant HTML5 casual games that load instantly and feel great to play.

  • Strong Cocos Creator or Phaser experience — we ship polished games fast.
  • A sharp sense of satisfying game feel, pacing and difficulty curves.
  • Comfort optimizing for mobile web: memory, texture and load time.

Frontend Engineer

Remote · Full-time

Own the portal experience — fast static pages, responsive UI and a smooth game-loading pipeline.

  • Modern vanilla JS and CSS with an eye for micro-interactions.
  • Performance mindset: Core Web Vitals and near-instant page loads.
  • Bonus: experience with SEO-driven content sites or static-site generation.

Game Designer (Casual / Puzzle)

Remote · Full-time

Design levels, mechanics and progression for our original casual and puzzle games.

  • Proven track record in casual, puzzle or match-3 level design.
  • Comfort with data-driven iteration: funnel, retention and level-balance tuning.
  • Strong prototyping skills to test ideas quickly.

Growth & SEO Marketer

Remote · Contract · US / JP

Drive organic growth for English and Japanese audiences through technical SEO, content and partnerships.

  • Hands-on with technical SEO, structured data and content strategy.
  • Experience growing game or entertainment sites from zero to meaningful traffic.
  • Native-level English or Japanese with strong writing skills.

Community & Content Manager

Remote · Part-time · US / JP

Grow and engage our player community across social channels, and craft content players actually enjoy.

  • Experience running communities on X, TikTok, Reddit or Discord.
  • A feel for short-form game content that travels.
  • Self-driven and comfortable wearing several hats.

Hiring Process

  1. Apply — send your CV, portfolio and a short note on why you'd like to join.
  2. Intro chat — a relaxed 30-minute call to align on the role and your goals.
  3. Work sample — a short take-home task (or a walkthrough of past work).
  4. Final interview and offer — usually within two weeks of your first chat.

FAQ

Where can I work from?

Anywhere with reliable internet. We just ask for a few hours of overlap with our core working window (UTC+8) for team syncs.

Do you hire freelancers or part-time?

Yes — marketing and content roles often start as contract or part-time arrangements, with room to grow into full-time.

What language do we work in?

English is our working language across the team. Japanese fluency is a big plus for JP-market roles.

How long does the process take?

From application to offer is typically two weeks. We keep it lean — no take-home marathons, no endless interview rounds.

Don't see your role?

We're always open to exceptional people. Send your CV and a short note on how you'd help — we read everything.
