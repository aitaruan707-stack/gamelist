About Nuts Snake

Steer a hungry little snake around the board, gobbling up nuts to grow longer while dodging the walls and your own tail.

Nuts Snake is optimized for touch and keyboard alike, loads in seconds and stays free to play forever — no download and no sign-up. If you enjoy casual, mobile, reaction games, you can play Nuts Snake online right now in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop.

How to Play

  1. Swipe or use the arrow keys to steer the snake.
  2. Eat the nuts to grow longer and raise your score.
  3. Avoid running into the walls or your own tail.
