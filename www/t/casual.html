casual games, one line each

Written from the full guide on each game's page. These titles are filed under Puzzle and Arcade.

  • Puzzle Yarn Fun — A colour-ordering puzzle played on a board of yarn-wound posts, with a knit-textured guide grid that starts at 0% — not the match-3 its catalogue blurb claims, and there is no cat anywhere on screen.
  • Puzzle: Water Sort — A liquid-sorting puzzle — the build calls itself "Hue & Brew" on screen — where coloured drinks have to be gathered into single bottles, with three charges each of shuffle, undo and a bottle tool.
  • Chroma Jam — A timed colour-clearing puzzle: tap blocks off a tray and send each colour to its own side rack, with five minutes on the clock.
  • Smash Blocks — A triple-match tile game: tap blocks out of a nine-by-nine grid whose centre three columns are stacked with symbols, into a three-cell tray, where three identical symbols vanish.
  • Bubble Safari — A straightforward bubble shooter with a real twist worth knowing about: the star meter fills from burst value, and every power-up is charged by watching an ad.
  • Spin Screw Jam — A screw-sorting puzzle: unscrew coloured screws from overlapping plates and get each colour into its matching box, with a short buffer rail you can extend.
  • Block Puzzle: Save Girl — An arrow-sequence puzzle with a dragon for a clock: lay arrows into a six-slot tray so a box reaches the girl, while a segmented dragon crawls the same road toward her.
  • Royal Matcher — A real match-3 on a nine-by-nine board: five tile designs, a two-part collection goal printed as flower 8 and castle 7, and a "Steps" budget of 22 on Level 1.
  • Arrow Maze Solve — A one-screen arrow-routing puzzle that teaches itself with three words — "Tap to Move!" — and runs a clock, a battery and three hearts at the same time.
  • Wizard Sort — Cast spells and sort magical potions in this enchanting sorting puzzle adventure. Brew, pour, and organize enchanted flasks across mystical levels.
  • Shift Dash Reac — A lane runner played on a four-lane road where the whole interface is a single column on the right: Turn, Shop, Sign In, and a skin you have already unlocked.
  • Satisfying Stack — A merge puzzle on a twelve-cell tray: choose a stack, then Push or Merge it, with a milestone track reading 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 and only five of the twelve cells open at the start.
  • Nuts Snake — Steer a hungry little snake around the board, gobbling up nuts to grow longer while dodging the walls and your own tail.
