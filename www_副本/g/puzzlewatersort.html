About Puzzle: Water Sort

Pour and sort colored water between bottles until each holds a single color. A calming liquid-sort puzzle with hundreds of handcrafted levels.

How to Play

  1. Tap a bottle, then tap another to pour the top color.
  2. A bottle can only pour onto the same color or an empty bottle.
  3. Sort every bottle to a single color to win.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Water Sort free?

Yes, completely free to play in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it is optimized for touch and mobile screens.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required — just press Play and it loads instantly.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved in your browser only, so clearing browser data may reset it.
