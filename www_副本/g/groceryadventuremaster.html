About Grocery Adventure: Master

Stock, sort and organize grocery shelves in this satisfying puzzle adventure. Tidy every aisle, master clever layouts and unlock lively new stores.

How to Play

  1. Tap a shelf, then tap another to move groceries.
  2. Group matching items to clear the shelf.
  3. Complete goals to unlock new stores.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Grocery Adventure: Master free?

Yes, completely free to play in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it is optimized for touch and mobile screens.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required — just press Play and it loads instantly.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved in your browser only, so clearing browser data may reset it.
